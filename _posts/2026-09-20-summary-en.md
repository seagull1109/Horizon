ChatGPT-6 Astra, a large language model, has successfully decoded a 108-year-old unsolved German code from World War I, revealing enemy movement intelligence. This breakthrough demonstrates the potential of AI in solving historical mysteries and could impact future decryption efforts. The model utilized advanced machine learning techniques to analyze historical documents and decrypt the code, which was previously undeciphered due to its complexity.
