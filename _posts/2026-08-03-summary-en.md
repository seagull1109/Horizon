## [Open letters about AI development](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/2/open-letters/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Microsoft led an open letter titled 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership' signed by 235 AI companies including NVIDIA, Amazon, and OpenAI, arguing against potential US government restrictions on open weight models. The letter was published on July 24th, 2026, and was followed by Anthropic's response and another letter called 'Pacing the Frontier' signed by 1,324 employees of frontier AI companies. This represents a significant industry pushback against potential government regulation of open source AI models, which could shape future AI development policies. The involvement of major tech companies and the contrasting positions taken by different organizations highlight the growing debate about AI safety, competition, and innovation in the United States. The Microsoft-led letter specifically supports distillation as a legitimate model development technique while arguing that open weight models allow broader community examination and improvement. Anthropic's response emphasized risks of authoritarian governments developing more powerful AI models and called for crackdowns on industrial-scale distillation operations, though they don't advocate for banning open weights models entirely.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 2, 04:16

**Background**: Open weight models refer to AI models where the learned parameters (weights and biases) are publicly released, allowing others to download, use, and potentially modify them. This contrasts with closed models where the parameters remain proprietary. The debate around open weight models centers on balancing innovation and competition against security concerns, particularly regarding potential misuse by malicious actors or foreign governments. The concept has become increasingly important as AI capabilities advance rapidly.
