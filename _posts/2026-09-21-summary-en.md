## [Mini-AGI Dynamic Continual Learning Model](https://github.com/volotat/mini-AGI/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

An open-source project introduces a dynamic continual learning model for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), trained on 8GB VRAM, with a focus on avoiding catastrophic forgetting and optimizing for small VRAM capacity. The project is significant as it represents a novel approach to AGI, potentially leading to more efficient and adaptable AI systems. It could impact the field of AI by offering a new paradigm for continual learning. The model utilizes a mixture of experts approach, allowing for dynamic addition and pruning of experts during training, and employs batch 1 training on a continuous stream of data to optimize VRAM usage.

## [Qwen Image 2.1 Update: Smaller Model with Native Transparency](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-image-2.1) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The Qwen Image 2.1 model has been updated with a smaller size of 7 billion parameters, a reduction from the previous 20 billion. It also introduces native transparency support, a feature not commonly found in open-weight models. The smaller size of Qwen Image 2.1 makes it more accessible for users with limited computational resources, while the native transparency support enhances its utility in various applications such as graphic design and product photography. The model's smaller size is achieved through a novel architecture that maintains high performance, and it supports both text-to-image generation and image editing. However, it uses a more restrictive license compared to previous versions.
